# Runtime Change: 0.5.6 — Preserve direct paths during forced relay

Version: 0.5.6

> [English](0.5.6.md) | [简体中文](0.5.6.zh.md)

## Problem

Direct mappings expire during forced relay, and retry controls are unavailable during ordinary cooldown.

## Recorded changes

<a id="patch-32"></a>
### Patch 32

0.5.6 keeps direct paths alive during forced pSp relay. Previously a force_relay peer sent no direct-socket traffic: its NAT opening expired and last_p2p aged. The first packet after cancellation hit the timeout/2 idle check, deleted the known entry as P2P_EXPIRED, and returned it to pending for full re-punching. Observed cancellation after four seconds restored direct connectivity immediately, but after 24 seconds fell back to pSp and re-punched. Send REGISTER every five seconds through the socket carrying the direct session, preferring the promoted worker. The round trip refreshes last_p2p/last_seen and NAT mappings on both sides. REGISTER_ACK from known peers also refreshes the local entry, which previously happened only during pending promotion. Cancelling forced relay therefore restores direct connectivity without re-punching.

<a id="patch-33"></a>
### Patch 33

0.5.6 offers “Retry P2P punching” from the friend-link context menu during ordinary pSp relay, including cooldown/inter-round waiting, rather than only in terminal failed state. set_peer_relay(false) resets both endpoints' failure budgets.
